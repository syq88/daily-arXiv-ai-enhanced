### [1] [HeadIO: Body-Frame Heading Decomposition for Neural Inertial Odometry on Head-Mounted Devices](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.14895)
*Taeyoung Yeon,Yiquan Li,Chenfeng Gao,Vasco Xu,Karan Ahuja*

Main category: cs.CV

TL;DR: HeadIO 面向头戴设备的纯惯性里程计（IMU-only IO），利用设备坐标系下的头部朝向分解与双向 Mamba 主干，在保持偏航不变性的同时解决倾斜导致的表征变化问题，以更低计算成本取得领先的 RTE-5s 表现。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 传统 IO 工作在重力对齐的世界坐标系中，不具备偏航等变性；偏航增强只能提升鲁棒性而非严格等变性，学习型等变层成本高。原始机体坐标系 IMU 对偏航天然不变，但头部倾斜会重新分配重力与运动信号，使表征随倾斜而变化。

Method: 提出头部惯性里程计 HeadIO：采用机体坐标系朝向分解，将信号分离为重力对齐分量与水平分量，仅依赖设备姿态中的倾斜分量实现倾斜不变性；同时使用双向 Mamba 作为时序主干。该方法轻量、可与任意主干组合，适合实时部署。偏航误差可通过单系数陀螺修正或稀疏外部偏航校正改善。

Result: 在四个头戴设备数据集上评估。在最大基准 Nymeria 上，HeadIO 的 RTE-5s 相比带学习型等变层的传统世界坐标方法提升 8.1%（0.260 m 到 0.239 m），同时 FLOPs 低近 3 倍、参数量少 8 倍。在 Aria 眼镜数据集和 Nymeria 的 IMU-only 设置中，HeadIO 在主要指标 RTE-5s 上均达到 SOTA。单系数陀螺修正可消除 11.6% 偏航误差并提升 RTE-5s 2.5%；每 30 秒一次稀疏偏航校正可提升 RTE-5s 12.0%。

Conclusion: HeadIO 证明了“机体坐标系 + 倾斜不变分解 + 高效序列建模”可在无相机头部跟踪中兼顾精度、偏航鲁棒性与低部署成本。其性能主要依赖倾斜角估计，而倾斜可从重力信号获得；偏航误差虽重要但可通过轻量或稀疏校正有效缓解。

Abstract: Inertial odometry (IO) using only Inertial Measurement Units (IMUs) enables camera-free head tracking in head-mounted devices such as VR headsets, AR glasses, and earbuds. Prior IO methods operate in a gravity-aligned world frame, which is not yaw-equivariant. Yaw augmentation improves robustness but does not guarantee equivariance; learned equivariant layers achieve it, but EqNIO's full pipeline costs nearly 3$\times$ the FLOPs of ours. While raw body-frame IMU inputs are inherently yaw-invariant, head tilt redistributes both gravity and motion signals across device axes, making the representation tilt-variant. We propose HeadIO, which combines a body-frame heading decomposition with a bidirectional Mamba backbone for head-mounted inertial odometry. Our decomposition achieves tilt invariance by separating gravity-aligned and horizontal components using only the tilt component of device orientation. We evaluate on four head-mounted datasets and find that HeadIO improves RTE-5s by 8.1% (0.260 m $\to$ 0.239 m) over world-frame methods with learned equivariant layers on Nymeria, the largest benchmark dataset, at nearly 3$\times$ lower FLOPs and 8$\times$ fewer parameters. HeadIO achieves state-of-the-art performance on our primary metric, RTE-5s, on all Aria-glasses datasets, which natively provide body-frame semantics for our decomposition method. We further evaluate the IMU-only deployment scenario on Nymeria, where HeadIO remains state-of-the-art. We show that our method relies mainly on tilt accuracy, which can be obtained from the gravity signal; for yaw, a single-coefficient gyroscope correction removes 11.6% of the yaw error and improves RTE-5s by 2.5%, and sparse external yaw corrections (for example, one every 30 s) can improve RTE-5s by 12.0%. Our decomposition is lightweight, composes with any backbone, and is suitable for real-time deployment on head-mounted devices.

### [2] [Adaptive Distance Detection and Adaptive Instance Segmentation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.15991)
*Kai Yi Ji*

Main category: cs.CV

TL;DR: 该论文研究第一人称射击游戏《Counter Strike: Condition Zero》中的敌人感知任务，比较YOLOv5s目标检测模型与自定义语义分割模型的推理性能，发现目标检测略快、语义分割对目标形状和轮廓更精确。针对模型复杂度提升可能导致速度差距扩大，论文提出自适应距离检测（Adaptive distance detection）融合两种算法，并进一步提出自适应实例分割（Adaptive instance segmentation）结合语义分割与全景分割。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 在FPS游戏环境或游戏AI/自动化辅助系统中，需要同时满足实时性和准确性的敌人识别。传统目标检测速度快，但通常只输出边界框，难以描述敌人的精确形状；语义分割能够提供像素级结果，轮廓更准确，但推理更慢。随着模型规模扩大，二者的延迟差距可能进一步放大，因此需要一种能兼顾速度与形状精度的融合检测方案。

Method: 作者在游戏场景中使用YOLOv5s训练目标检测模型，用于检测敌人；同时定制语义分割网络，进行像素级敌人识别。训练完成后，比较两类模型的推理时间。论文进一步提出Adaptive distance detection，根据目标距离或场景复杂度动态选择或组合目标检测与语义分割；并提出Adaptive instance segmentation，将语义分割与全景分割结合，以改善实例级感知能力。

Result: 实验观察表明，YOLOv5s目标检测在推理速度上略快于语义分割模型，而语义分割在捕捉物体精确形状方面表现更好。论文指出，如果两种模型的复杂度继续增加，它们之间的推理速度差异可能显著扩大。提出的自适应检测方法旨在缓解这一矛盾，但摘要中未给出详细的定量指标，例如mAP、IoU、FPS或不同距离阈值下的性能。

Conclusion: 单一目标检测或语义分割难以同时满足FPS场景中敌人识别对实时性和形状精度的要求。通过距离自适应方式融合检测与分割，以及结合语义分割与全景分割的实例化方法，有望在速度、准确性和复杂模型扩展性之间取得更好的平衡。

Abstract: This paper is about object detection model and semantic segmentation model in an FPS game Counter Strike: Condition Zero to detect enemies. Semantic segmentation is pixel-wise detection algorithm. We customized the architecture for semantic segmentation. We used YOLOv5s to train object detection model. After training, the inference time of semantic segmentation was compared to object detection. It was observed that object detection was slightly faster and semantic segmentation is slower but better at detecting the exact shape of the object. However, we found out that this slight difference of inference can grow exponentially if the both models get more complex. To solve this, we propose a novel approach called Adaptive distance detection which combines both algorithms. We will also introduce Adaptive instance segmentation to combine semantic segmentation and panoptic segmentation.
