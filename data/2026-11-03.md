### [1] [SACQ: Structure-Aware Codebook Quantization for Ultra-Low-Bit Vision-Language-Action Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.41408)
*Haesung Lee,Insu Han*

Main category: cs.CV

TL;DR: 本文提出SACQ，一种面向视觉-语言-动作（VLA）模型的超低比特仅权重量化框架，通过结构感知码本拟合、动作感知混合比特分配和二阶段量化后优化，在显著压缩模型的同时保持策略动作性能，并提升部署效率。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: VLA模型虽能支持复杂机器人行为，但参数量和显存占用巨大，难以部署到资源受限平台。传统源自大语言模型的后训练量化（PTQ）方案在VLA的超低比特场景下性能退化明显，现有低比特PTQ方法往往忽略下游计算结构，并且未将比特分配与最终动作误差联系起来。

Method: 作者提出SACQ，核心包括三点：（1）结构感知码本拟合，采用角色相关的二次度量，结合输入统计和下游敏感性刻画量化目标；（2）动作感知混合比特分配，根据逐层压缩对最终动作误差增加量的影响动态分配比特；（3）二阶段量化后优化，利用二阶误差补偿重新分配码字索引，并基于解码后的动作校准码本和行组尺度。

Result: 在GR00T N1.7上，SACQ在每语言/动作权重1.5比特的条件下，于四个LIBERO套件取得93.85%平均成功率，同时将模型内存降低4.83倍；结合INT8激活量化和低精度注意力后，成功率为93.55%，推理速度较BF16提升1.51倍。在SimplER上，2比特权重量化在Visual Matching和Variant Aggregation中分别达到63.76%和52.27%成功率，对比BF16的58.90%和53.45%，在部分设置中甚至优于全精度基线。

Conclusion: SACQ表明，面向VLA的超低比特量化不能简单沿用LLM的PTQ范式，而需要同时考虑网络结构、动作敏感性和量化后补偿。该方法能在极低位宽下显著压缩VLA模型并加速推理，同时保持甚至提升机器人策略成功率，具有实际部署价值。

Abstract: Vision-Language-Action (VLA) models enable capable robot behavior, but their large memory footprint limits deployment on resource-constrained robotic platforms. Weight quantization is a natural remedy, yet post-training quantization (PTQ) recipes inherited from large language models degrade when applied to VLAs at ultra-low precision. Existing low-bit PTQ methods often fail to explicitly account for the structure of downstream operations and allocate precision without considering its effect on the policy actions. We introduce SACQ, an ultra- low-bit weight-only vector-codebook quantization framework for VLAs. SACQ combines (1) structure-aware codebook fitting with role-specific quadratic metrics that reflect input statistics and downstream sensitivity; (2) action-aware mixed-bit allocation based on the increase in final-action error as individual layers are compressed; and (3) two-stage post-quantization refinement that reassigns codeword indices with second-order error compensation and calibrates codebooks and row-group scales against the decoded action. Using GR00T N1.7, SACQ achieves 93.85% average success across four LIBERO suites at 1.5 bits per language/action weight while reducing model memory by 4.83x. With INT8 activation quantization and low-precision attention, it retains 93.55% success while running 1.51x faster than BF16. On SimplER, SACQ further achieves 63.76% and 52.27% success under Visual Matching and Variant Aggregation at 2 bits per language/action weight, compared to 58.90% and 53.45% for BF16, respectively.

### [2] [Mechanistic Interpretability for End-to-End Self-Driving](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.41185)
*Manav Gagvani,Benjamin Namikas,Sivamurugan Velmurugan,Nysa Kumar,Shrey Sharma,Thomas Peterson,Pratyush Mathur,Hyunseo Chang,Rohan Paleja*

Main category: cs.RO

TL;DR: 本文使用稀疏自编码器对单摄像头端到端自动驾驶路径规划模型进行机制可解释性分析，发现其内部激活可形成与路线意图、车速和刹车行为相关的可解释稀疏特征，并能通过反事实图像编辑和潜变量操控预测地影响规划输出，且可用于降低轨迹选择错误。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 端到端自动驾驶模型虽然规划能力强，但内部决策过程高度不透明，限制了对系统的可观测性、安全审计和信任建立。因此需要一种方法能够解析模型内部表示，并验证这些表示是否与真实驾驶语义和可干预行为相关。

Method: 作者在一个简单的单摄像头端到端路径规划模型上训练稀疏自编码器，提取内部激活的稀疏潜变量。随后通过无监督聚类分析这些潜变量，识别与路线意图、车辆速度和刹车行为相关的结构；利用成对反事实图像编辑检验特定特征是否受到交通灯状态、停车标志等视觉场景因素的系统性影响；并通过单独操控 SAE 潜变量，观察其对横向位移、曲率和刹车等规划输出的因果影响；最后基于 SAE 潜变量训练轻量适配器用于轨迹选择。

Result: SAE 潜变量的无监督聚类能够以 98.3% 的准确率恢复路线意图，无需显式监督。部分 SAE 特征在反事实图像编辑下对交通灯状态和停车标志存在表现出可解释的视觉关联。操控单个 SAE 潜变量会使规划输出产生可预测的单调变化，例如横向位移、曲率和刹车行为发生变化。基于 SAE 潜变量训练的轻量适配器将轨迹选择误差降低了 3.44%，优于不使用 SAE 潜变量的同规模适配器。

Conclusion: 稀疏自编码器可以揭示端到端自动驾驶模型内部具有语义意义且可干预的表示，为机制可解释性分析提供了实用路径。结果表明，稀疏表示不仅有助于理解模型如何编码路线、速度和刹车等驾驶概念，也能提升下游规划任务的可解释性能，从而增强端到端自动驾驶系统的可观察性和可信度。

Abstract: End-to-end (E2E) autonomous driving models achieve strong planning performance but remain largely opaque, limiting observability and trust. We apply mechanistic interpretability to E2E driving by training Sparse Autoencoders (SAEs) on the internal activations of a simple, single-camera end-to-end path planning model. Unsupervised clustering of SAE latents reveals interpretable structure aligned with route intent, vehicle speed, and braking behavior, recovering route intent with 98.3% accuracy without supervision. Through paired counterfactual image edits, we further show that specific SAE features are visually grounded and systematically respond to scene changes such as traffic light state and stop sign presence. Steering individual SAE latents produces predictable, monotonic changes in planner outputs including lateral displacement, curvature, and braking. Finally, we demonstrate a practical application of these representations: we build a lightweight adapter trained on SAE latents that reduces trajectory selection error by 3.44%, outperforming an equivalent adapter that omits SAE latents, confirming that the sparse representation captures useful information for interpretability.
