### [1] [InfMasking: A Unified Variational Framework for Synergistic Multimodal Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.07702)
*Liangjian Wen,Qun Dai,Jiang Duan,Yong Dai,Jianzhuang Liu,Dongkai Wang,Jun Wang,Fuyuan Cao,Zenglin Xu,Zhao Kang*

Main category: cs.CV

TL;DR: InfMasking提出一种基于“无限掩码”的统一多模态学习框架：在融合前随机遮蔽各模态特征，并要求不同掩码下的融合表示与参考目标保持一致，从而迫使模型学习真正依赖多模态协同的信息，而非单纯依赖主导模态或模态间共享信息。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 多模态模型的目标是利用只有在多个模态共同出现时才可获得的协同信息。然而实际融合模块常可通过主导模态或模态间重复共享的信息满足训练目标，导致模态间的交互与协同未被有效利用，降低模型鲁棒性和可解释的交互学习能力。

Method: 该方法在融合前对每个模态的特征随机进行掩码，使每次采样形成不同的跨模态部分证据组合，并得到对应的融合表示。所有掩码融合表示被要求与一个参考目标保持一致：无监督训练时参考未掩码的融合视图，有标签时参考任务标签对应的类别代理。为处理对无限掩码采样的期望目标，文章将掩码融合表示建模为高斯分布，并优化闭式变分界，用掩码诱导的协方差控制其与总体矩差异。

Result: 在评测基准上，InfMasking不仅提升了预测性能，还在学到的模态专属表示中提高了基于PID的任务标签协同信息估计，同时在训练后的预测器中表现出更高的InterSHAP交互归因，说明模型更有效地利用了跨模态交互信息。

Conclusion: InfMasking通过掩码一致性与无限掩码期望下的概率建模，抑制了多模态融合中对主导模态或共享捷径信息的依赖，促进模型学习在部分观测下仍有意义且可解释的跨模态交互模式，为多模态协同学习提供了统一框架。

Abstract: Multimodal models are expected to exploit synergistic information, that is, information available only when multiple modalities are observed together. In practice, however, a fusion module can fit the training objective by relying on a dominant modality or on information that modalities share, and the synergy between modalities is often left unused. We propose InfMasking, a unified multimodal learning framework based on Infinite Masking. Before fusion, InfMasking randomly masks the features of each modality; each sampled mask produces a different partial combination of cross-modal evidence and a fused representation of the corresponding modality interactions. All masked fused representations are required to remain consistent with a reference target: the unmasked fused view when training is unsupervised, and the class proxy of the task label when labels are available. Maintaining this consistency across diverse partial combinations discourages reliance on a dominant modality or on shared information alone, and drives the fusion module to learn cross-modal interaction patterns that remain meaningful under partial observations. Since the objective is an expectation over infinitely many masks, we model the masked fused representations as Gaussian and optimize closed-form variational bounds, whose population-moment gaps are controlled by the masking-induced covariance under this model. On the evaluated benchmarks, InfMasking improves predictive performance and yields higher mean PID estimates of task-label synergy in learned modality-specific representations, alongside higher InterSHAP interaction attribution in the trained predictors. Code is available at this https URL.

### [2] [Fast and Accurate Hand Estimation in Pixels, Interaction and World Space](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.07711)
*Jinkun Cao*

Main category: cs.CV

TL;DR: FAH 是一组面向单目手部网格估计的快速且准确的模型，通过同时预测 2D/3D 关节与表面点并拟合 MANO/MHR-Hand，在几何精度、图像对齐、接触估计和世界空间跟踪方面优于基线，并可达到高帧率推理。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 现有单目手部网格估计器通常预测手部刚性参数并将网格投影为 2D 关键点；作者发现直接预测的 3D 关节与 2D 预测更一致，且用预测点拟合网格能降低网格误差。因此希望构建更准确、更快、可估计手部部件与接触、并能在无物体几何信息时推理的单目手部估计模型。

Method: 方法以 SAM 3D Body 的手部分支为基础，通过微调与特征蒸馏构建 FAH 模型族。模型同时预测对应的 2D 关节、3D 关节和表面点，并据此拟合 MANO 或 MHR-Hand 网格；可选地估计解剖学手部部件和手-物接触，而推理时不需要物体几何。还引入因果细化以降低加速度误差，并结合相机估计将手部结果放入世界空间。

Result: 实验表明 FAH 在图像空间对齐、手部几何、接触估计以及世界空间跟踪上相较所评估基线有所提升。默认 ViT-L 模型在 RTX 4070 Ti SUPER 上带网格拟合可达 65 fps，启用部件与接触时为 49 fps；最小 ViT-S 模型在不启用网格拟合、部件和接触时可达 125 fps。

Conclusion: FAH 证明了直接预测多模态手部点并进行网格拟合比仅依赖参数投影更有效，可在单目手部估计中同时获得较高精度、实用效率和扩展能力；代码已公开。

Abstract: Monocular hand mesh estimators commonly predict rig parameters and project the mesh to obtain 2D keypoints. We find that directly predicted 3D joints agree better with 2D predictions, and fitting a mesh to predicted points reduces mesh error. We present FAH (Fast and Accurate Hand Estimation), a family of models built on SAM 3D Body's hand branch through fine-tuning and feature distillation. The models predict corresponding 2D and 3D joints and surface points, fit MANO or MHR-Hand, and optionally estimate anatomical parts and contact without object geometry at inference. Causal refinement reduces acceleration error, and camera estimation places the hands in world space. Experiments show improvements in image-space alignment, hand geometry, contact estimation and world-space tracking over the evaluated baselines. The default ViT-L model runs at 65 fps with mesh fitting on an RTX 4070 Ti SUPER, or 49 fps with parts and contact enabled. The smallest ViT-S model reaches 125 fps without mesh fitting, parts or contact. Code: this https URL.

### [3] [PlayGen2: Learning Real-Time Playable Worlds with Explicit 3D State](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.07867)
*Jihai Zhang,Yuhan Zhou,Junyou Li,Deheng Ye,Qi Dou,Yu Cheng*

Main category: cs.CV

TL;DR: 该论文提出PlayGen2：一种实时交互式世界模型，将持久化的3D世界状态学习与神经渲染解耦，以解决生成式视频中空间与时间一致性难以长期维持的问题。模型在Doom环境中实现几何感知的玩家动作预测、NPC交互、零样本地图泛化、多人交互组合与可编程玩法，并达到约160ms后端延迟。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 现有可交互世界生成若仅以生成帧作为循环世界表示，会把世界状态绑定到视角相关的外观合成上，难以维护长期空间结构、持久几何和动态实体一致性。构建“可玩游戏世界”需要响应式控制、持久空间结构和与动态实体的连贯交互，因此需要显式的世界状态表示而非仅依赖视频帧记忆。

Method: PlayGen2维护一个持久世界记忆，包含静态几何的体素占据栅格，以及以身份索引的玩家和其他实体3D状态。在固定大小的局部区域中，世界状态模型联合学习动作条件下的实体动态、几何补全和实体发现，并将更新提交到持久记忆。另设神经渲染器，仅根据世界状态生成的深度图和语义投影合成第一人称观测，不把渲染图像反馈回状态预测，从而解耦世界状态演化与视觉生成。

Result: 在Doom测试平台上，模型成功学习包含几何感知玩家运动和玩家—NPC交互在内的世界动态。完整模拟与渲染管线在MacBook Pro M4 Pro上实现约160ms后端延迟，具有较高计算效率。模型还可零样本迁移到未见过的Doom地图，通过组合单人模型实现多人交互，并支持基于规则状态干预的可编程玩法。代码与预训练权重已开源。

Conclusion: 将显式、持久的3D世界状态与神经渲染分离，是构建可控、一致且可交互世界模型的有效路径。PlayGen2证明学习型游戏世界模型可在实时性、空间持久性、动态交互、泛化和可扩展性方面取得较好平衡，为交互式世界建模和可编程游戏智能体提供了可行框架。

Abstract: Building a playable world requires more than generating plausible video: it demands responsive control, persistent spatial structure, and coherent interactions with dynamic entities. Using generated frames as the sole recurrent world representation ties these requirements to view-dependent appearance synthesis, making it difficult to maintain long-term spatial and temporal consistency in generation and interaction. We present PlayGen2, a real-time interactive world model that separates learned 3D state evolution from neural rendering. PlayGen2 maintains a persistent world memory comprising static geometry represented by a volumetric occupancy grid and identity-indexed 3D states for the player and other entities. Within a fixed-size local region, its world-state model jointly learns action-conditioned entity dynamics, geometry completion, and entity discovery, committing updates to a persistent world memory. A separate neural renderer synthesizes first-person observations from depth and semantic projections of the world state, without feeding generated images back into state prediction. Using Doom as a testbed, we demonstrate that the model successfully learns world dynamics including geometry-aware player motion and player--NPC interactions. The framework is computationally efficient, enabling the complete simulation and rendering pipeline to achieve approximately 160ms backend latency on a MacBook Pro with an M4 Pro chip. Beyond single-player interaction, we demonstrate zero-shot deployment on unseen Doom maps, multiplayer interaction through the composition of single-player models, and programmable gameplay through rule-based state interventions. These capabilities demonstrate the transferability and extensibility of the learned game-world model. Code and pretrained model weights are publicly available.

### [4] [Reviving the Discarded High-Resolution Feature for Transformer-Based Tiny Object Detection](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.07994)
*Zehao Wang,Si Wu,Guiping Cao,Xin Li,Yaowei Wang*

Main category: cs.CV

TL;DR: 针对小目标检测中高分辨率细节易丢失、计算开销大的问题，提出ReDHF框架，通过复活被丢弃的高分辨率特征、跨尺度融合、浅层监督与查询交互，显著提升小目标检测性能。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 小目标仅占据极少像素，特征下采样过程中细粒度信息容易丢失。DETR类检测器通常因高分辨率特征计算代价高而将其丢弃，但简单重新引入高分辨率特征又会带来密集背景噪声，影响小目标检测效果。

Method: 提出ReDHF框架，包含三个协同模块：1）跨尺度可变形融合CSDF，以较低计算成本构建语义增强的高分辨率特征，并将细粒度细节注入浅层特征；2）椭圆引导的浅层监督EGSS，通过几何自适应的辅助监督，引导增强特征学习更可靠的分类与定位先验；3）全尺度查询交互FSQI，使解码器查询同时获取底层细节和高层语义信息，从而聚合更丰富的小目标线索。

Result: 在多个DETR变体基线上取得一致性能提升，并在AI-TOD-v2、VisDrone和SODA-D等基准上达到SOTA。最佳变体相对于基线分别提升+1.4 AP、+1.6 AP和+1.5 AP，尤其在极小目标和较小目标上提升明显。

Conclusion: 复活高分辨率特征并抑制背景噪声、补充几何先验与增强查询信息交互，是提升小目标检测的有效途径。ReDHF具有较好的通用性和可扩展性，可作为DETR类检测器中增强小目标细节感知的有效框架。

Abstract: Tiny object detection remains challenging because tiny instances occupy only a few pixels, and their fine-grained details are easily lost during feature downsampling. Although the highest-resolution feature preserves rich local details for tiny objects, DETR-like detectors often discard it due to its large computational cost, while directly reintroducing this feature also brings dense background noise. To address this problem, we propose ReDHF, a novel framework that Revives the Discarded High-resolution Feature for tiny object detection. ReDHF has three cooperative components. Cross-Scale Deformable Fusion (CSDF) first constructs a semantically enhanced highest-resolution feature and injects its fine-grained details into the shallow feature under a moderate cost. Ellipse-Guided Shallow Supervision (EGSS) then guides the enhanced feature to learn more reliable classification and localization priors by applying geometry-adaptive auxiliary supervision. Full-Scale Query Interaction (FSQI) further enables decoder queries to access both low-level details and high-level semantic information, allowing them to aggregate richer cues for tiny objects. Extensive experiments show that ReDHF consistently improves multiple baselines across different DETR variants and achieves state-of-the-art performance on the evaluated benchmarks. The best ReDHF variants surpass their baselines by +1.4 AP on AI-TOD-v2, +1.6 AP on VisDrone, and +1.5 AP on SODA-D, with especially notable gains on tiny and small objects.

</details>

### [5] [Aligning Policy Encoders with a Shape Teacher](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.08275)
*Madelon Bernardy,Mohammad Mahdi Derakhshani,David M Knigge,Riccardo Valperga,Artem Moskalev*

Main category: cs.RO

TL;DR: 该论文提出“形状教师对齐”（shape-teacher alignment），在训练阶段用预训练的3D形状表示（Point-MAE特征）对齐图像策略编码器的patch特征，使纯图像输入的机器人操作策略获得3D几何结构信息，从而加速策略收敛并提高成功率，同时部署时不需要3D/深度输入或额外网络结构。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 3D感知表示对视角、物体位置和遮挡变化更具鲁棒性，有助于机器人操作，但部署时需要额外传感器和处理流程。相比之下，基于图像的策略更实用。作者希望解决：是否能在训练时让图像策略学习3D形状信息，同时保持纯图像接口，以便部署时无需额外3D输入？

Method: 提出训练时的形状教师对齐机制。使用预训练3D形状表示（Point-MAE特征）作为目标，将每个独立物体的3D特征对齐到图像策略编码器中对应的图像patch特征。该机制只在训练阶段引入教师信号，不改变图像策略的输入形式、网络架构或部署流程，从而把几何结构蒸馏进图像表示中。

Result: 在LIBERO基准上，形状教师对齐使图像表示更围绕物体形状和部件结构组织，并显著提升训练效率。对于预训练策略，Object套件收敛加速4.7倍，Spatial套件收敛加速5.6倍。策略性能和成功率得到改善，并且部署时无需使用形状教师或深度输入。

Conclusion: 训练时的3D形状对齐可以在不增加部署成本的情况下，将3D几何知识注入纯图像机器人操作策略中，提高表示的结构化程度、加快收敛并增强操作性能，为实用化机器人图像策略提供了一种有效的表示学习方法。

Abstract: 3D-aware representations can improve robotic manipulation by providing geometric information that is robust to changes in viewpoint, object position, and occlusion. However, using 3D inputs at deployment requires additional sensing and processing, making image-based policies more practical. We ask whether an image-based policy can acquire 3D shape information during training while retaining a purely image-based interface at deployment. We propose shape-teacher alignment, a training-time mechanism that uses a pretrained 3D shape representation as an alignment target for an image-based policy encoder. Specifically, we use Point-MAE features from individual objects as targets for corresponding image patches, transferring geometric structure to the policy without changing its deployment inputs or architecture. On LIBERO, shape-teacher alignment produces image representations that are more structured around object shape and corresponding parts, while substantially accelerating policy convergence and improving success rates. For pretrained policies, convergence is accelerated by 4.7x and 5.6x on the Object and Spatial suites, respectively, without requiring the shape teacher or depth input at deployment.

### [6] [Social Humanoid: Socially Responsive Human-Humanoid Interaction](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.08407)
*Vongani H. Maluleke,James Ni,Kie Horiuchi,Lea Wilken,Jiahui Lei,Evonne Ng,Roei Herzig,Jitendra Malik,Trevor Darrell,Angjoo Kanazawa*

Main category: cs.RO

TL;DR: 本文提出 Social Humanoid，一个分层式人形机器人系统，使 29 自由度 Unitree G1 能够根据人类伙伴的动作生成并执行具有社会响应能力的全身运动；同时构建了一个 260 小时的人类—人形机器人交互（HHI）基准数据集。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 现有人形机器人通常被训练为独立智能体，缺乏与人类伙伴进行物理协调和相互预测、适应的能力。该工作旨在解决人形机器人在真实社会互动中无法理解、响应并配合人类动作的问题。

Method: 系统采用分层结构：高层策略使用共享的离散 token 表示，对人类和人形机器人动作进行灵活序列建模，既生成反应式机器人动作，也在人类被遮挡时预测其运动；低层全身跟踪控制器负责将生成动作执行到真实机器人上。训练数据来自五个动作捕捉数据集，提出两阶段自适应重定向方法：将一个交互伙伴重定向到机器人，同时对另一伙伴进行形变以保留接触动力学、可达性和相对体型。

Conclusion: 该研究将社会交互能力引入人形机器人控制，提出了一种面向人类—人形机器人协作的分层建模与数据重定向方案，并通过新基准验证了其在真实交互任务中的可行性。

Abstract: Humanoid robots are typically trained as solitary agents, leaving them unable to coordinate physically with human partners who naturally anticipate and adjust to one another. To bridge this gap, we present Social Humanoid, a hierarchical system that generates and executes socially responsive whole-body motion on a 29-DoF Unitree G1 conditioned on a human partner's motion. To train the system, we introduce a two-phase adaptive retargeting procedure that retargets one interaction partner to the robot while morphing the other partner to preserve contact dynamics, reachability, and relative body scale. We apply this procedure across five motion-capture datasets to produce a new Human-Humanoid Interaction (HHI) benchmark of 260 hours of human-humanoid interactions. Our results demonstrate that Social Humanoid produces coordinated, human-aligned behavior across diverse interaction types in simulation and the real world. Project page: this https URL
