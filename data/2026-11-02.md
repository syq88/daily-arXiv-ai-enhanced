### [1] [DyCraft: An Agentic System for Long-Horizon 4D Scene Generation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.40754)
*Jeff Tan,Hang Dai,Zeqian Long,Yanzhe Lyu,R. Kenny Jones,Shangzhe Wu,Chen Geng,Jiajun Wu*

Main category: cs.CV

  <summary>Details</summary>
