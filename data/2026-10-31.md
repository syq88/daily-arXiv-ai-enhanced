### [1] [Calibrating Track-Query Updates at Test Time for Multi-Object Tracking](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.38494)
*Riku Inoue,Shogo Sato,Yu Mitsuzumi,Kazuhiko Murasaki,Tomoyasu Shimada,Takashi Sano*

Main category: cs.CV

TL;DR: 该论文针对查询传播式多目标跟踪中的身份切换问题，发现跟踪查询在身份切换前易受到邻近活动轨迹方向的更新影响，并提出训练无关、梯度无关的测试时轨迹查询校准方法 TQC，以抑制邻近轨迹干扰并提升跟踪指标。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 在多目标跟踪中，当外观相似的轨迹发生重叠或交叉时，查询传播式跟踪器虽然能够跨帧传递目标表示，但仍容易出现身份切换。作者怀疑当前帧的查询更新可能包含指向邻近活动轨迹的分量，从而在后续帧中干扰身份关联，因此需要分析跟踪查询更新与身份切换之间的关系，并寻找推理阶段可执行的校准方法。

Method: 首先，论文分析身份切换前发生错误的轨迹查询更新，观察到三个现象：传播的轨迹查询表示在身份切换前变化更剧烈；当前帧更新在其他活动轨迹方向上的正投影更大；当后续身份切换涉及的活动轨迹在切换前仍存在时，可利用边界框 IoU 高召回地找到对应轨迹。随后，论文提出测试时轨迹查询校准方法 TQC。TQC 是训练无关且梯度无关的推理阶段方法，仅使用推理时已有的轨迹查询表示和边界框信息，对即将传播的跟踪器输出进行有界校正，以削弱更新中指向邻近活动轨迹的不利分量。

Result: 在 DanceTrack 和 SportsMOT 的九个测试设置上，TQC 在所有设置中均提升了 HOTA 和 AssA，并在八个设置中提升了 IDF1；平均 HOTA 提升 +0.38，同时只带来有限的推理开销。在六个验证设置上的受控实验表明，与校正幅度相匹配但方向不同的替代方案相比，TQC 的校正方向能带来更一致的 HOTA 改进。

Conclusion: 身份切换与轨迹查询更新中指向邻近活动轨迹的分量密切相关，利用推理阶段可获取的查询表示和边界框信息，通过 TQC 进行有界校准可以在不重新训练、不改变网络结构的情况下改善查询传播式多目标跟踪器的身份关联性能。

Abstract: Multi-Object Tracking (MOT) requires localizing multiple objects and maintaining their identities over time. Recent query-propagation trackers carry target-specific representations across frames and update them with current-frame image features, yet identity association remains difficult when visually similar targets overlap or cross. During such interactions, current-frame query updates may contain components directed toward nearby active tracks, which may affect identity association in later frames. To investigate this issue, we analyze track-query updates preceding identity switches (IDSWs), where the same target is assigned a different track ID. Our analysis shows that (i) propagated track-query representations change more abruptly before IDSWs, (ii) current-frame updates have larger positive projections along directions toward other active tracks, and (iii) bounding-box intersection over union (IoU) retrieves the counterpart track involved in a subsequent IDSW with high recall when that track remains active before the switch. Motivated by these findings, we propose Test-time Track-Query Calibration (TQC), a training-free and gradient-free method for query-propagation trackers. TQC calibrates the tracker output before propagation using a bounded correction estimated from update components directed toward nearby active tracks. It requires only track-query representations and bounding boxes available during inference, without retraining or architectural modification. Across nine DanceTrack and SportsMOT test settings, TQC improves HOTA and AssA in all settings and IDF1 in eight, with an average HOTA gain of +0.38 and limited inference overhead. Controlled variants on six validation settings further show that TQC's correction direction yields more consistent HOTA improvements than alternative directions with matched correction norms.

### [2] [Joint Rigid Alignment and Morphable Reconstruction from Partial 3D Face Scans](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.39161)
*Eli A. Underwood,Nicolas Rondan,Angel V. Peterchev,J. Matias Di Martino*

Main category: cs.CV

TL;DR: 本文提出一个基于GeoTransformer超点匹配架构的端到端网络，在单次前向传播中同时完成部分真实3D人脸扫描的刚体位姿估计与形状补全，并显著优于多种非刚性配准基线。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 将部分真实3D人脸扫描对齐到规范表示时，刚体位姿估计与未观测区域形状补全本质耦合；现有流程通常把点云配准和后续非刚性拟合或变形分开处理，难以利用二者之间的联合约束，容易导致对齐误差累积或形状补全失败。

Method: 方法在GeoTransformer超点匹配框架上构建单一网络，利用transformer点特征和预测的粗对应关系，通过交叉注意力回归器将部分扫描映射到离线学习的3D可变形人脸模型的分块系数；随后在同一前向过程中生成补全后的morphed模板，并以其为目标进行细粒度Sinkhorn匹配和刚体位姿估计。训练仅依赖与ground-truth morphed几何的几何对齐监督，不需要人工标注的人脸模型系数。

Result: 在留出身份数据集上，相比六种经典与学习型非刚性配准基线，该方法最强基线的逐顶点误差低3到4倍；对于将头部塌缩到可见区域的基线，误差改善超过一个数量级。在NoW和NPHM两个独立真实扫描数据集上，该方法同时具有最高精度和最快运行速度。

Conclusion: 将刚体位姿估计与形状补全统一在端到端框架中，可更有效地解决部分3D人脸扫描到规范几何的对齐问题，并提升配准精度与效率。

Abstract: Aligning a partial, real-world 3D face scan to a canonical representation requires solving two coupled problems at once: estimating the rigid pose that brings the scan into a common frame, and completing the facial shape in regions the scan never observed. Existing pipelines typically decouple these steps, running a generic point-cloud registration front end followed by a separate non-rigid fitting or deformation stage. Building on the GeoTransformer superpoint-matching architecture, we propose a single network that predicts both a rigid pose and a shape-complete point cloud in one forward pass: a cross-attention regressor, conditioned on transformer point features and predicted coarse correspondences, maps a partial scan to per-patch coefficients of a 3D morphable face model learned offline from a corpus of 8,000 identities. The resulting morphed template is then used, in the same forward pass, as the target for fine-grained Sinkhorn matching and rigid pose estimation, supervised purely by geometric alignment to ground-truth morphed geometry, so no hand-labeled coefficients are required. Against six classical and learned non-rigid registration baselines on held-out identities, our method leads the strongest ones by 3-4 times on per-vertex error and baselines that collapse the head onto the visible region by more than an order of magnitude, and is simultaneously the most accurate and fastest method on ground-truth-free scans from two independent real-scan datasets, NoW and NPHM. Code will be made publicly available.

### [3] [Cross-Encoder Anchor Refinement for Zero-Shot Anomaly Detection](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.39727)
*Kaisong Ji,Ziyang Xu,Chang Bian,Yu Zhou,Wenyu Liu,Xinggang Wang*

Main category: cs.CV

TL;DR: 提出Cross-Encoder Anchor Refinement（CEAR），通过视觉编码器交互和跨注意力锚点精化，在原型构建前融合上下文与局部证据，从而提升零样本异常检测与细粒度定位能力，并在13个工业与医学数据集上取得领先表现。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 零样本异常检测需要将辅助训练域的异常判别能力迁移到未见类别，并定位细微缺陷。关键难点在于构建兼具上下文表征和互补局部证据的判别性参考；现有方法在anchor阶段可能缺乏对不同视觉编码路径的有效交互，导致细粒度定位不足。

Method: CEAR连接同一图像的两条视觉编码路径：一条为预训练用于工业产品识别的卷积编码器，一条为ViT。卷积编码器构建图像专属记忆，ViT不同层的空间anchor通过跨注意力查询该记忆，并进行残差精化。随后，图像条件下的正常与异常查询门控anchor聚合，构建原型并用于异常评分。

Result: 在13个工业和医学数据集上，CEAR在68项图像级检测与像素级定位评测中有57项取得最佳性能；在44项像素级比较中超越其他SOTA方法41项。在11个定位数据集上，像素级最大F1和平均精度分别平均提升4.45和3.79个百分点。

Conclusion: 在anchor阶段引入跨编码器交互能有效整合互补视觉表征，提升跨域零样本异常检测尤其是细粒度像素级定位性能，说明早于原型构建阶段融合上下文与局部证据是重要且有效的设计策略。

Abstract: Zero-shot anomaly detection aims to transfer anomaly discrimination from auxiliary training domains to unseen categories while localizing subtle defects. A key challenge is to construct discriminative references that combine contextual representations with complementary local evidence. We present Cross-Encoder Anchor Refinement (CEAR), which connects two visual encoding paths for the same image at the anchor stage, before normal and anomalous prototypes are constructed. An independent convolutional encoder pretrained for industrial product recognition builds an image-specific memory shared across selected vision transformer (ViT) layers. Spatial anchors read from ViT patch features query this memory through cross-attention and undergo residual refinement. Image-conditioned normal and anomalous queries then gate anchor aggregation to construct prototypes for anomaly scoring. Across 13 industrial and medical datasets, CEAR achieves the best performance in 57 of 68 evaluations spanning image-level detection and pixel-level localization. Notably, CEAR surpasses all other state-of-the-art methods in 41 of 44 pixel-level comparisons. Across 11 localization datasets, mean gains in pixel-level maximum F1 score and average precision are 4.45 and 3.79 percentage points, respectively. This study shows how cross-encoder interaction at the anchor stage can integrate complementary visual representations for fine-grained anomaly localization across domains.
